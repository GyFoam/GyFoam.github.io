GyFoam

Stiffness & Morphing

Overview

GyFoam offers two key features that set it apart from traditional foam materials: customizable stiffness and morphing capabilities. These properties enable the creation of adaptive, responsive structures that can change their mechanical behavior based on design parameters and environmental conditions.

In this page, we explore how GyFoam achieves these unique properties through its innovative material composition and structural design, and demonstrate various applications that leverage these capabilities.

Foaming and Expansion

Thermal Expansion Mechanism

GyFoam's core functionality is based on the thermal expansion of embedded microspheres. When heated to activation temperature (typically 150°C), these microspheres expand dramatically, increasing their volume by up to 50-70 times.

This expansion mechanism creates a unique foam structure with:

  • Controlled porosity based on TEM concentration
  • Uniform cell distribution throughout the silicone matrix
  • Significant volume increase while maintaining structural integrity
  • Permanent shape change after cooling

Customizable Stiffness

Concentration-Dependent Properties

The stiffness of GyFoam can be precisely controlled by adjusting the concentration of Thermal-Expandable Microspheres (TEM) in the silicone matrix. Higher TEM concentrations result in greater expansion during the heating process, creating more air pockets within the structure and reducing the overall density.

Our research demonstrates a clear correlation between TEM concentration and the resulting mechanical properties:

  • Low concentrations (5-15%): Higher density, greater stiffness, reduced expansion
  • Medium concentrations (15-25%): Balanced properties, moderate expansion
  • High concentrations (25-40%): Lower density, reduced stiffness, maximum expansion

Structural Design Influence

Beyond material composition, the structural design of GyFoam significantly impacts its mechanical properties. Our lattice-based approach allows for precise control over stiffness through:

  • Cell size and geometry: Smaller cells create denser structures with higher stiffness
  • Wall thickness: Thicker walls increase structural rigidity
  • Lattice pattern: Different patterns (hexagonal, triangular, etc.) offer varying mechanical responses
  • Gradient structures: Varying cell sizes throughout the object create stiffness gradients

By combining material concentration control with structural design parameters, GyFoam enables unprecedented customization of mechanical properties within a single object.

Applications of Customizable Stiffness

The ability to precisely control stiffness enables numerous applications across various fields:

  • Ergonomic Products: Customized cushioning with varying stiffness zones for optimal comfort and support
  • Protective Equipment: Impact-absorbing structures with tailored energy dissipation properties
  • Medical Devices: Patient-specific supports and orthotics with biomechanically optimized stiffness profiles
  • Soft Robotics: Actuators and grippers with precisely engineered mechanical responses

Morphing Structures

Programmable Deformation

By strategically varying the concentration of TEMs throughout the structure, we can program specific deformation patterns that emerge during the heating process. This enables:

  • Controlled bending and twisting motions
  • Transformation from flat to three-dimensional structures
  • Sequential unfolding based on activation temperature gradients
  • Complex shape changes that would be difficult to achieve with traditional manufacturing

The deformation behavior can be predicted and simulated using computational models, allowing designers to create precisely engineered morphing structures.

Applications of Morphing Structures

The morphing capabilities of GyFoam enable innovative applications across multiple domains:
